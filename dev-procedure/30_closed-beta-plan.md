# 30 — Closed Beta / Dogfooding
**Purpose**: Validate in real usage.  
**Inputs**: Build + cohort list.  
**Outputs**: KPI readouts, top issues, feedback log.

## Checklist
- Define beta cohort & consent
- Success metrics & thresholds
- Feedback channels & triage
- Weekly KPI review & decisions

**Definition of Done**: KPIs trending to targets or fixes planned.
