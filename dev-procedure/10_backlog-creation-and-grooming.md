# 11 — Backlog Creation & Grooming
**Purpose**: Convert scope to thin vertical slices.  
**Inputs**: FRDs, design.  
**Outputs**: User stories, acceptance criteria, estimates, priorities.

## Checklist
- Story map of end-to-end flow
- Slice into increments deliverable in ≤1 sprint
- Write stories with AC (Gherkin)
- Tag non-functionals (perf, a11y, security)
- Prioritize by risk/value

**Definition of Done**: 2 sprints ready to pull.

### AI Prompt
As a PM/TL pair, generate a prioritized backlog for **{{feature set}}**.  
Produce: story map, user stories with acceptance criteria (Gherkin), estimates (S/M/L), dependencies, and labels (a11y/perf/security). Aim for thin vertical slices. Output as Markdown tables + bullets.
