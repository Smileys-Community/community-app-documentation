# 2 — Product Requirements Document (PRD)
**Purpose**: Define outcomes, scope, and non-goals.  
**Inputs**: Brief, research.  
**Outputs**: PRD with KPIs, acceptance criteria, constraints.

## Checklist
- Goals & non-goals
- North-star metric + KPI tree
- Scope (MVP capabilities) & assumptions
- Target users/personas
- High-level user stories
- Constraints & dependencies

**Definition of Done**: PRD baselined and versioned.

### AI Prompt
You are a PM. Draft a PRD for **{{your product}}**.  
Include: goals/non-goals, target personas, north-star metric + KPI tree, success criteria (with thresholds), MVP scope (capabilities & exclusions), top risks & mitigations, dependencies, and high-level user stories. Output in Markdown with a summary table at the top.
