# 06 — Team Setup
**Purpose**: Establish team shape, ownership, and rituals.  
**Inputs**: Scope & timeline.  
**Outputs**: RACI, DRIs, Definition of Ready/Done, working agreement.

## Checklist
- Roles & DRIs (PM, TL, EM, Design, QA, Data, SRE)
- RACI per workstream
- Cadence (planning, standup, retro, demo)
- DoR/DoD
- Channels & tooling (tracker, design, docs, incident)

**Definition of Done**: Cadence, boards, comms channels live.

### AI Prompt
You are an engineering manager. Create a Team Setup guide for **{{project}}**.  
Include: org chart (roles and DRIs), RACI by workflow (discovery, delivery, release), working agreements, ceremonies (agenda & timeboxes), Definition of Ready/Done, tool stack (Jira/Linear, Git, CI/CD, docs), and escalation paths. Output as a concise Markdown handbook.
