# 1 — Market Research
**Purpose**: Validate problem, map landscape, size opportunity.  
**Inputs**: Seed brief.  
**Outputs**: Competitor matrix, TAM/SAM/SOM, interview guide + notes.

## Checklist
- 5–15 problem interviews (target segment)
- Competitor scan (features, pricing, distribution, moat)
- Market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM) + approach assumptions
- Regulation/compliance scan (if applicable)
- Risks & unknowns

**Definition of Done**: Must-have pains & target segments are clear.

### AI Prompt
Act as a market analyst. For **{{your idea}}** aimed at **{{target segment}}**, produce:  
1) Competitor/alternative matrix (columns: product, ICP, core features, pricing, strengths, weaknesses, distribution, moat),  
2) Market sizing with TAM/SAM/SOM and assumptions,  
3) Top 10 insights from 10 hypothetical customer interviews (problems, triggers, objections),  
4) Risks and unknowns to validate next.  
Return as Markdown tables and bullets.
