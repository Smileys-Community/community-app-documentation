# 01 — Create Seed Product Brief
**Purpose**: Frame the problem, target user, value prop, and “why now.”  
**Inputs**: Idea notes, any prior insights.  
**Outputs**: 1–2 page brief, success hypotheses, constraints (budget/timeline).  

## Checklist
- Problem statement (1–2 sentences)
- Target user & top pain(s)
- Value proposition & differentiation
- Why now (timing, tech, regulation, trends)
- Constraints (budget, timebox, compliance)
- Success hypotheses (qual + quant)

**Definition of Done**: Stakeholders align on scope & goals; brief versioned.

### AI Prompt
You are a senior product strategist. Create a 1–2 page Seed Product Brief for **{{your idea}}**.  
Include: problem statement, target user/persona, top pains, value proposition, why now, key assumptions, risks, constraints (budget/time/regulatory), success hypotheses (with measurable signals), and open questions. Output in crisp Markdown with headings and bullets.
