# 05 — UX Design Research
**Purpose**: Derisk usability and value.  
**Inputs**: PRD/FRDs, target users.  
**Outputs**: Personas/JTBD, task analysis, research report.

## Checklist
- Research goals & hypotheses
- Method (interviews/usability tests/surveys)
- Screener & discussion guide
- Findings, themes, quotes
- Recommendations & design risks

**Definition of Done**: Top usability risks + mitigations identified.

### AI Prompt

1) Research plan (goals, hypotheses, method, sample, timeline),  
2) Screener and discussion guide,  
3) Expected measures (task success, SUS, time on task),  
4) Reporting template,  
5) Risk register of usability uncertainties.  
Return in Markdown.
