Events are time-boxed. Usually ~2h30mins, with an option to extend.
We take breaks spontanously as the need arise. 

# Recap (~5m)
Events shall start with a quick ~5 minute recap on the previous event's topics. 

# Homework Delivery & Overview  (~10m)
Everyone sends a summary of their work to the channel, 
and we'll have a general overview before we dig into individual items. 

## Homework Summary Template
- What went well
- What was challenging? (if any)
- Any other highligts you'd like to add. (tips and tricks, questions, etc.) - keep it concise 
- - Feel free to add screenshots of your progress. 

# Planning (~10min) 
- Items to discuss
- Items to develop


# Discussions on the delivered work (~25m)
We can dig into 1 or 2 subjects to overcome challenges or improve the delivered work.

# Hands on Practice (~45m) 
Develop new features, fix bugs, etc.
Take notes and share feedback. 


# Retrospective on the live practice (~30m)
What went well?
What did we learn?
What still puzzles you? -if any.
what would you do differently next time? 


# Homework assignment (~10m)
Could be new features, bug fixes, qa, code reviews, etc.
