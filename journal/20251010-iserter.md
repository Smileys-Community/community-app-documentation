
# Welcoming new members and growing

We talked about having more discipline and commitment to a better defined purpose. 
Part of the discussion involved questioning what types of incentive we could offer to newcomers. 
Why should they commit and stick around? the thought process lead to the discussion of organizational structures, handling expectations, and so on. 

Along the way, I thought of a comparison matrix I had seen couple years ago, and I just had ChatGPT recreate it.  Perhaps it may come in handy in the next discussions, so I'm adding it to the repo. 


## Organizational Models

| name | structure | primary_actors | motive_force | leadership_force | primary_leadership | processes | focus | decision_policy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Traditional Management | hierarchy | managers and workers | business need | management authority | top-down | linear/waterfall | control and alignment | leader decides |
| Agile Methods | cross-functional teams | peer team members | user need | product vision or external change | distributed | sprint-based | speed to customer impact | advice process |
| Sociocracy 3.0 | heterarchy of circles | peers, roles | driver | consent | distributed | emergent/adaptive | effective collaboration culture | consent |
| Holacracy | hierarchy of circles | roles | tension | transparent rules/constitution | distributed | emergent/adaptive | rules and role autonomy | integrative decision-making |
| Team Topologies | platform/enabling + stream-aligned | peer team members | user need | product vision or external change | distributed | continuous flow/kanban | speed to customer impact | advice process |
| Platform Cooperative | network of small cells | participants/community | community objective | owner/member mandate | representative (delegated) | emergent/adaptive | member value and inclusion | consent |
| Cooperative (general) | heterarchy of circles | participants/community | community objective | owner/member mandate | representative (delegated) | informal/continuous | member value and inclusion | majority vote |
| Steward-owned (Purpose Trust) | cross-functional teams | contributors and stewards | mission/vision/aims | elected stewards | distributed | iterative | effective collaboration culture | advice process |



## Terms & Definitions (optional)

### name
| Name | Description |
|---|---|
| Traditional Management | Command-and-control with clear managerial hierarchy and centralized decisions. |
| Agile Methods | Iterative product development (Scrum/Kanban) emphasizing customer value and feedback. |
| Lean | Reduce waste, improve flow, and continuously improve via small, disciplined changes. |
| Sociocracy 3.0 | Consent-based governance with circles, explicit domains, and driver-focused change. |
| Holacracy | Role-based operating system using a constitution, tensions, and structured meetings. |
| Spotify Model | Squads, tribes, chapters, and guilds to scale autonomous teams with craft alignment. |
| Team Topologies | Stream-aligned teams supported by platform/enabling/complicated-subsystem teams. |
| Lattice | Minimal formal hierarchy; influence via commitments and reputation across the network. |
| Cooperative | Member-owned organization prioritizing member benefit and democratic control. |
| Steward-owned (Purpose Trust) | Mission-locked company where control and profits are bound to purpose, not exit. |
| Community of Practice | Groups organized to learn, standardize, and share craft across boundaries. |
| Adhocracy | Flexible, project-based organizing that forms and dissolves around opportunities. |
| Ambidextrous Organization | Separate units for exploration and exploitation with coordinated leadership. |
| Platform Cooperative | Digital platform owned by its users/providers with shared governance and surplus. |

### structure
| Name | Description |
|---|---|
| hierarchy | Single chain of command with levels of authority and reporting lines. |
| divisional (business units) | Semi-autonomous units by product, customer, or region, each with functions inside. |
| matrix | Dual reporting lines (e.g., functional and product/region) for coordination. |
| cross-functional teams | Stable teams with multiple disciplines owning outcomes end-to-end. |
| communities of practice | Overlay of craft/discipline groups for learning and standards. |
| hierarchy of circles | Nested circles with clear domains and double-linking (e.g., Holacracy style). |
| heterarchy of circles | Peer circles connected by agreements/links rather than formal nesting (S3 vibe). |
| network of small cells | Many autonomous cells loosely coupled by shared principles and protocols. |
| platform/enabling + stream-aligned | Stream teams own value streams, platform/enabling teams reduce their cognitive load. |

### primary_actors
| Name | Description |
|---|---|
| managers and workers | Managers plan/decide, workers execute; classic separation of roles. |
| roles | Work anchored in explicit role accountabilities rather than job titles. |
| peers | Individuals act as equals, coordinating via agreements and facilitation. |
| peer team members | Teams of equals own outcomes; team-level commitments drive action. |
| participants/community | Members participate as stakeholders; legitimacy comes from member benefit. |
| leaders and workers | Leadership sets direction, contributors execute with local autonomy. |
| contributors and stewards | Contributors create value; stewards safeguard standards and mission. |

### motive_force
| Name | Description |
|---|---|
| business need | Revenue, margin, growth, or risk constraints drive priorities. |
| user need | Observed customer problems/opportunities steer work selection. |
| community objective | Shared community aims (inclusion, learning, mutual aid) motivate action. |
| mission/vision/aims | Stated purpose and long-term aims pull the org toward desired states. |
| tension | Holacracy term: a sensed gap between current reality and a potential improvement. |
| driver | S3 term: situation + impact + need; the explicit reason to act. |
| OKRs/KPIs | Quantified goals and measures prioritize initiatives. |
| regulatory requirement | External rules/standards mandate changes or controls. |

### leadership_force
| Name | Description |
|---|---|
| management authority | Formal positional power directs and approves work. |
| product vision or external change | A compelling product/market pull or environmental shift leads decisions. |
| community consensus | Broad agreement among members legitimizes direction (often slower, inclusive). |
| consent | Decisions proceed unless there is a reasoned, paramount objection (Sociocracy). |
| transparent rules/constitution | Codified rules (e.g., Holacracy Constitution) define authority and processes. |
| advice process | Anyone may decide after seeking advice from experts and the affected. |
| elected stewards | Mandated representatives steward domains on behalf of members. |
| owner/member mandate | Owners or member-owners set boundaries and elect leadership. |

### primary_leadership
| Name | Description |
|---|---|
| top-down | Direction chiefly flows from leaders to the organization. |
| bottom-up | Initiatives originate from contributors/teams and gain adoption. |
| distributed | Authority is delegated to roles
