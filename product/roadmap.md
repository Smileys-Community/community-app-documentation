# Roadmap

## MVP (Launch)
- Public site, Auth + Social, RBAC, Groups & Events CRUD
- Event detail w/ map, RSVP, QR tickets, Check‑in
- Profiles & settings, transactional emails, OG metadata
- Legal pages, basic analytics, mobile‑first UI

## v1.x (Fast Followers)
- Personalized “Events for Me”
- Daily/weekly digests (configurable)
- Event suggestions by trusted members (review/approve)
- Search (groups & events)
- Polymorphic Abuse Reports
- Reputation basics (counts), group badges surface
- Admin analytics dashboard

## v2 (Scale & Moat)
- Vetting workflow; private groups & invite links
- Reputation score & automated badges
- Group/Event chat; DMs + policy matrix
- Mobile push notifications
- Advanced moderation & trust/risk scoring
