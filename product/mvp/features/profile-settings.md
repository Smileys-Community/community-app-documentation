# Feature Requirements — Profile & Settings (MVP)

## Goals
Give users a central place to view tickets and manage account.

## User Stories
- As a user, I can view my upcoming/past events and my tickets.
- As a user, I can change my email and password, or delete my account.

## Acceptance Criteria
- “My Events” lists upcoming/past; “My Tickets” shows QR & status.
- Edit email (re-verify), change password.
- Delete account (soft-wipe user and revoke tokens; irreversible warning).

## Non-Functional
- Mobile-first; protect PII; confirm critical actions.
